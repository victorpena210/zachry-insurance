TEXAS CAREERS

What Does A Life Insurance Agent Career In Texas Involve?

Life insurance agents help families understand protection needs, compare available options, complete applications, and stay in touch throughout the process.

Short answer: Zachry Insurance is recruiting motivated Texas candidates who are willing to learn the business, meet applicable licensing requirements, communicate clearly, and follow up consistently. You can express interest before you are licensed.

Help Families Understand Coverage

Ask questions, listen carefully, and explain life insurance options in clear language based on each family's situation.

Build A Consistent Pipeline

Follow up with prospects, referrals, and people who requested information. Consistency matters more than one big sales day.

Keep Learning

Continue building knowledge about products, underwriting basics, client communication, compliance, and carrier processes.

Texas licensing requirements can change. Review our Texas life insurance licensing guide and verify current requirements with the official Texas Department of Insurance resources linked there.

PART-TIME PATH

Can You Start As A Part-Time Life Insurance Agent?

A part-time start may be possible for people who want to explore insurance while keeping another job or managing other responsibilities.

Potentially. Part-time does not mean casual. Candidates still need consistent time for licensing, training, calls, appointments, follow-up, and client service. Availability and contracting requirements can vary, so discuss your schedule with Clay before making plans.

Protect Focused Time

Set aside predictable weekly blocks for studying, calls, appointments, training, and follow-up.

Set Clear Expectations

Be realistic about availability and respond reliably when a prospect or client is expecting you.

Build Skills Gradually

Use each week to improve product knowledge, conversation skills, organization, and confidence.

TRAINING & MENTORSHIP

What Training Do New Life Insurance Agents Receive?

New agents do not need to know everything on day one. They need a clear learning path, realistic expectations, practice, and ongoing coaching.

Training may include: licensing direction, client-conversation practice, product education, follow-up habits, compliance awareness, appointment flow, and ongoing coaching as you build experience. Training does not guarantee licensing, sales, or income results.

Licensing Direction

Understand the basic Texas path and use official state resources to verify current exam, fingerprint, application, and continuing-education requirements.

Conversation Skills

Practice asking better questions, listening carefully, explaining options clearly, handling objections, and following up professionally.

Product Confidence

Learn how life insurance products are commonly used for family protection needs while following carrier and compliance requirements.
