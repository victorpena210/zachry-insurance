HOW WHOLE LIFE COVERAGE WORKS

Protection Designed To Last A Lifetime

Whole life insurance is permanent coverage designed to remain in force for life when required premiums are paid and policy terms are met. It can be used to leave a death benefit for the people you choose.

Level Premium

Many traditional whole life policies have a premium designed to remain level rather than increasing as you get older.

Lifetime Coverage

Unlike term insurance, whole life coverage does not end after a selected number of years when policy requirements continue to be met.

Cash Value

A whole life policy may build cash value over time. Loans or withdrawals can reduce the policy's cash value and death benefit.

CHOOSING A COVERAGE AMOUNT

Start With The Final Arrangements You Prefer

Think about whether you prefer cremation or burial, then review local prices and any other bills or responsibilities you do not want to leave to your family.

Cremation Planning

A benefit of $10,000 or less may help cover many cremation-related costs, depending on the services selected and prices in your area.

Burial Planning

A benefit of $20,000 or less may help with burial services and related final expenses, depending on the arrangements selected.

Other Final Expenses

Consider medical bills, travel, legal expenses, small debts, or time away from work that your loved ones may need to manage.

These coverage amounts are planning examples, not guarantees. Funeral prices and family needs vary, so review current local costs before choosing a benefit amount.
